3. Service description and sources

ChangeWatch helps monitor changes on publicly accessible web pages: product pages, public collections or categories, prices, promotions, offers, availability and delivery terms. Confirmed important changes trigger an email alert containing a source link. When no significant alert has been sent, a weekly summary reports on monitoring activity.

Alerts support decision-making. The customer must independently verify information before any important business decision. No economic outcome or competitive advantage is guaranteed.

4. Compatibility and coverage

Detection depends on the publicly accessible information on each monitored page. Compatibility and coverage are checked during setup. Dynamic structures, JavaScript-loaded content, access restrictions and anti-bot mechanisms may limit or prevent monitoring. Some collection or category pages allow only partial coverage.

The service does not guarantee compatibility with every website, complete catalogue coverage or detection of every change. False positives and missed changes may occur. A page’s compatibility may change after activation. Pages requiring authentication or the circumvention of an access control are not supported.
