How do I know whether my pages are compatible?

Compatibility is not universal. Public accessibility and page structure determine what can be monitored. We check compatibility and coverage during setup. Some websites restrict automated access; we may suggest another public page instead. Pagination and extraction limits can restrict the portion of a collection we monitor: one URL does not guarantee coverage of the entire catalogue.

Which pages can we monitor together?

Public URLs: product pages, collections, pricing, promotions or delivery terms. We check compatibility during setup. Pages that require a login are not supported.

What happens after payment?

Choose your plan and subscribe directly through Stripe Checkout. We then work with you to finalise the pages to monitor, their compatibility and coverage, and the included manual setup. If you have a question before paying, you can use the contact form; it is not required to subscribe. Payment does not set up monitoring automatically.

Does my team need to install anything?

No. Setup is carried out manually with you. You receive alerts by email, with no software or browser extension to install.

How often are pages checked?

Once a day with Starter and Business, and up to three times a day with Pro. The agreed frequency is confirmed during setup. This is not real-time monitoring.

Which changes does ChangeWatch detect?

Price changes, new or removed products, promotions, delivery terms, availability and offers, depending on what is visible on the monitored pages. Detection of every change is not guaranteed.

What if nothing changes?

We do not send artificial alerts. When no significant alert has been sent, a weekly summary confirms that monitoring is active and reports on its activity.

Can I change the URLs being monitored?

Yes. Contact us to replace a page, within your plan’s URL limit. We check the compatibility of new pages before adding them.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Plans are monthly, with no minimum term, and you can cancel at any time. Email changewatch@cybersignal.fr to request cancellation. Your request stops future renewals; the service remains available until the end of the paid period, with no automatic refund unless required by law or expressly agreed. Please read the Terms and Conditions.

Does ChangeWatch guarantee that all competitor information is accurate?

No. Information depends on the source pages and may change or contain errors. Always check the source before an important business decision. ChangeWatch supports monitoring and does not guarantee business results.
